## 2. Event Description <a name="event-description"></a>

### What Happened
Perplexity reviewed DHR-0007 and recommended tightening the "Potential Action" field to include Artifact Type, Priority, and Compile Later? for better triage after shifts.

### Why It Matters (First Principles)
A reliable input layer requires:

- Fast capture in the field
- Consistent structure for later processing
- Clear triage signals for compilation decisions
- Minimal friction to prevent skipped entries

The refined template balances speed with structure. Too much structure slows capture; too little makes compilation inconsistent.

### User Observation
> "DHR-0008 should feel like a refinement, not a redesign."

The journal stays fast. The template just makes triage easier later.
