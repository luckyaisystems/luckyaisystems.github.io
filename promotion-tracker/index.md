## Why This Tracker Exists
Promotion is not luck — it is accumulated evidence.

This tracker converts:

- daily notes  
- weekly compilations  
- patterns  
- case studies  
- DHR contributions  

into **structured promotion signals**.

It aligns directly with:

- Equinix operational expectations  
- CFT‑IV readiness  
- long‑term Critical Facilities career progression  

---

## Tracker Components
- **Milestones** — What you must achieve  
- **Progression** — How your role and salary evolve  
- **Evidence** — What proves your capability  
- **Templates** — Standardized formats for consistency  

---

## How To Use This Tracker
1. After each week, update **weekly-evidence.md**  
2. After each case study, update **case-study-evidence.md**  
3. After each DHR, update **dhr-evidence.md**  
4. Review milestones monthly  
5. Review progression quarterly

## Promotion Philosophy
Promotion is earned through:

- consistency  
- clarity  
- capability  
- evidence  

**“Capability compounds when evidence is captured.”**
