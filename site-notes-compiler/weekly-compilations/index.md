# Site Notes Compiler — Weekly Compilations

**Purpose:** Convert daily notes into structured weekly intelligence for DHRs, case studies, and capability tracking.

---

## Why Weekly Compilations Matter

Weekly synthesis is the **feedback layer** of the Knowledge Compiler. It transforms:

- Raw notes → structured insights
- Incidents → patterns
- Patterns → case studies
- Case studies → promotion evidence

This is where learning compounds.

---

## Weekly Workflow

1. Review all notes from the week
2. Identify:
   - Recurring patterns
   - Notable incidents
   - System interactions
   - Operator decisions
3. Summarize the week using:
   - **pattern-extraction-template.md**
   - **case-study-template.md**
4. Store the weekly output here
5. Link to:
   - Patterns
   - Systems
   - Incidents
   - DHRs
   - Readiness rubric
