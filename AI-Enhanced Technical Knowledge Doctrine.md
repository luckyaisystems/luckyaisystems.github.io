<h2 id="purpose">1. Purpose</h2>

This doctrine defines how AI will be used to accelerate technical learning, compress capability timelines, and build a long-term knowledge retrieval engine for Building Systems Engineering, Critical Facilities Operations, and Data Centre Infrastructure.

It reframes AI from **study assistance** into technical knowledge extraction, manual interpretation, and operational insight generation.

This document serves as a foundational philosophy for how LuckyAISystems approaches learning, documentation, and capability development.

---

<h2 id="core-principle">2. Core Principle: AI as a Technical Knowledge Retrieval Engine</h2>

AI does **not** replace learning.

AI removes friction from accessing, extracting, and reusing technical knowledge.

You will interact with:

- UPS manuals
- Generator manuals
- Chiller manuals
- CRAC documentation
- BMS manuals
- Electrical switchgear documentation
- ASHRAE standards
- CSA standards
- Seneca lecture notes
- Seneca lab sheets
- Vendor documentation
- Equinix SOPs and MOPs

AI transforms these documents into:

- Instant answers
- Extracted procedures
- Summarized concepts
- Cross-linked insights
- Actionable capability

---

<h2 id="verification-doctrine">3. Verification Doctrine</h2>

AI accelerates understanding but does not replace verification.

Technical decisions affecting safety, equipment, compliance, or operations must always be confirmed against:

- Official equipment manuals
- Manufacturer documentation
- CSA standards
- ASHRAE standards
- Employer SOPs, MOPs, and EOPs
- Instructor guidance where applicable

> **AI is an interpreter, not an authority.**

<h2 id="knowledge-classification">5. Knowledge Classification</h2>

### Public Knowledge

Safe to upload into third-party AI systems:

- Textbooks
- Lecture notes
- Public vendor manuals
- ASHRAE publications
- CSA publications (where permitted)
- Public research papers

### Private Knowledge

Never upload into third-party AI systems unless explicitly permitted by company policy:

- Equinix SOPs
- MOPs
- EOPs
- Internal diagrams
- Customer information
- Access procedures
- Proprietary documentation
